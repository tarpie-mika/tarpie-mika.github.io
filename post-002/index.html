Books eh?

Oct 19, 2026: I read a book

A couple months ago, or maybe more like half a year ago by now if memory serves, my girlfriend and I went to watch a film on a whim because we did not know what to do that evening. We just looked up what was showing at the time, and picked something that seemed interesting. Most if it was "eeeeh", but this one film caught my eye. I had heard good things about it, but in all honesty i knew next to nothing. We didn't even watch the trailer before hand. We trusted out gut, got tickets for the next showing, and got out the door.

Project Hail Mary

amaze amaze amaze



We went in with basically no expectations, and what we got was better than I had imagined. I love this movie and I have cried every time I have watched it so far (twice in the cinema, and twice at home). I loved the humanity that this film portrais, the hopefulness. "But Mika, this is a movie", I hear you say, and it is. But the film was so great that it actually made me interested in reading! I know right, wild stuff. But to me it was quite a big deal, because even if I have read quite a few books in my life, I had not picked one up in actual years. The last time I picked up a book, and it really captured me, was when the third entry in The Hunger Games trilogy had just came out. I read all three books in a matter of days. That might have been because I was on vacation and had nothing better to do, but it still counts!

Reading PHM was like a breath of fresh air. The last couple of books I had tried to read was something like Lord of the Rings or A Song of Ice and Fire, and while I adore the LotR films, I just could not finish it for whatever reason. That's not because they were bad, I'm sure, but maybe the style of writing just was not for me?
