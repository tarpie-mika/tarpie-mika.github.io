			A couple months ago, or maybe more like half a year ago by now if memory serves, my girlfriend and I went to
			watch a film on a whim because we did not know what to do that evening. We just looked up what was showing at the time,
			and picked something that seemed interesting. Most if it was "eeeeh", but this one film caught my eye. I had heard good things
			about it, but in all honesty i knew next to nothing. We didn't even watch the trailer before hand. We trusted out gut, got tickets
			for the next showing, and got out the door.

					<div class="boxPink" style="padding-bottom: 5px;">
						<h1 style="margin-top: -5px;"><u>Project Hail Mary</u></h1>
						<h3 style="margin-top: -25px;">amaze amaze amaze</h3>
						<img src="/homeMedia/placeholderBanner.png" width="700">
						<br><br>

						We went in with basically no expectations, and what we got was better than I had imagined. I love this film and I have 
						cried every time I have watched it so far (twice in the cinema, and twice at home). I loved the humanity that this 
						film portrais, the hopefulness. "But Mika, this is a movie", I hear you say, and it is. But the film was so great 
						that it actually made me interested in <b>reading</b>! I know right, wild stuff. But to me it was quite a big deal,
						because even if I have read quite a few books in my life, I had not picked one up in actual years. The last time I 
						picked up a book, and it really captured me, was when the third entry in The Hunger Games trilogy had just came out.
						I read all three books in a matter of days. That might have been because I was on vacation and had nothing better to do,
						but it still counts! <br><br>

						Reading PHM by Andy Weir was like a breath of fresh air. The last couple of books I had tried to read was something like Lord of the 
						Rings or A Song of Ice and Fire, and while I <b>adore</b> the LotR films, I just could not finish the first book for whatever reason.
						That's not because it was bad, but maybe the style of writing just was not for me? But Project Hail Mary? I could not put it down. 
						For me that still meant taking breaks from reading, but only because I have a hard time comitting to a task on any given day.
						Reading PHM made me <b>want</b> to read for the first time in years. And i cried, both from laughing and from sadness. When I 
						eventually finished the book, I just wanted to read more of the same. I had rarely been left wanting like that after finishing 
						a piece of written media. I immediately picked up another book I had been meaning to read: Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep 
						(which i borrowed from a friend of mine over a year ago at this point... thanks Kupo). And it was amazing too. I then picked 
						up The Martian, also by Mr. Weir, and for this one I have not watched the film beforehand. I am excited and intrueged, and I have 
						a new found love for reading. I can't wait to see where this one takes me. <br><br>

						For Reigniting my love for the written word,<br>
						Thank you PHM. Thank you Andy Weir.
