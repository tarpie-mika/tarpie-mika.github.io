						<h1 style="margin-top: -5px;"><u>Finding myself</u></h1>
						<h3 style="margin-top: -25px;">I want tiddies...</h3>

						I began exploring my sexuality during Gymnasium (roughly equivalent to highschool I believe), but I hadn't really 
						been with anyone other than a single girl at the time. It was during this time, at about the age of 16-17, that I realised 
						I liked more than just girls. But I never really told anyone, as I was still figuring it out. I still did not know I was 
						trans, or that "being trans" was really a thing. I finished Gymnasium still not knowing. I had heard of "shemales" through... 
						online means, sure, but it never really clicked for me. I moved back home with my parents before starting uni.

						During the next year or so, I lived at home while working. With the money I earned from working as a caretaker at Det Frie Gymnasium, 
						I started buying stuff to experiment more and figure out what this feeling was. I was so anxious my parents would find my toys, 
						quite a relatable experience I think. I don't think I ever told my girlfriend at the time. I tried once, but I think she was so tired, 
						that when morning came she couldn't remember it. After a year of on/off work and just, being around, I started uni at the Technical 
						University of Denmark. 
						
						During the start of the second semester at uni, January 2020, my girlfriend broke up with me. Then covid hit. Roughly a month 
						later I moved to a dormitory in Herlev; I finally had space completely to myself, and I didn't need to hide my stuff anymore! 
						I slowly started experimenting more with a feminine side I hadn't explored before: nail polish, shaving regularly, 
						removing my body hair, stuff like that. And while I can't remember how or what happened, during this "femboy era" I realised that 
						there might be more to it, and I started using other pronouns among my queer friends, along with a different name. It felt good. 
						I then went to the doctor to get started in the Danish healthcare system...
						<br><br><br><br>


						<h1 style="margin-top: -5px;"><u>Who I am</u></h1>
						<h3 style="margin-top: -25px;">woah I have tiddies now!</h3>

						So the Danish healthcare system is famous for being essentially free, but its trans care is also, <i>not the best</i>. It is 
						certainly better than what they have elsewhere, but it is not just informed consent, what a nightmare. But after a year of waiting, 
						meetings, and coming out to my parents (to great success thankfully), I finally got on oestrogen. At the age of 25. 
						<br><br><br><br>


						<h1 style="margin-top: -5px;"><u>Feeling wanted</u></h1>
						<h3 style="margin-top: -25px;">woah she has tiddies now!</h3>

						Even before I started oestrogen, as soon as I started identifying as transfem, I found a cornucopia of queer people, and for 
						some reason, a lot more interest in me. I don't quite get it, cus it was ... a lot. For someone like me who was never really 
						wanted by others (as far as i know at least), it seemed like I just needed to find my fellow queers. Suddenly I felt like I 
						belonged, and I could feel that people wanted me there. It was weird.
