I finally know who i am

Growing up I knew something wasn't quite right. At the age of ten I already knew I wanted something to be different. I prayed to a god that I did not even believe in, that I would wake up the next day as a girl. But it never happened. And I slowly forgot about it, believing it to be an impossible thing to change. Until at the age of about 22...

Finding myself

I want tiddies...

I began exploring my sexuality during Gymnasium (roughly equivalent to highschool I believe), but I hadn't really been with anyone other than a single girl at the time. It was during this time, at about the age of 16-17, that I realised I liked more than just girls. But I never really told anyone, as I was still figuring it out. I still did not know I was trans, or that "being trans" was really a thing. I finished Gymnasium still not knowing. I had heard of "shemales" through... online means, sure, but it never really clicked for me. I moved back home with my parents before starting uni.

During the next year or so, I lived at home while working. With the money I earned from working as a caretaker at Det Frie Gymnasium, I started buying stuff to experiment more and figure out what this feeling was. I was so anxious my parents would find my toys, quite a relatable experience I think. I don't think I ever told my girlfriend at the time. I tried once, but I think she was so tired, that when morning came she couldn't remember it. After a year of on/off work and just, being around, I started uni at the Technical University of Denmark.

During the start of the second semester at uni, January 2020, my girlfriend broke up with me. Then covid hit. Roughly a month later I moved to a dormitory in Herlev; I finally had space completely to myself, and I didn't need to hide my stuff anymore! I slowly started experimenting more with a feminine side I hadn't explored before: nail polish, shaving regularly, removing my body hair, stuff like that. And while I can't remember how or what happened, during this "femboy era" I realised that there might be more to it, and I started using other pronouns among my queer friends, along with a different name. It felt good. I then went to the doctor to get started in the Danish healthcare system...



Who I am

woah I have tiddies now!

So the Danish healthcare system is famous for being essentially free, but its trans care is also, not the best. It is certainly better than what they have elsewhere, but it is not just informed consent, what a nightmare. But after a year of waiting, meetings, and coming out to my parents (to great success thankfully), I finally got on oestrogen. At the age of 25.



Feeling wanted

woah she has tiddies now!

Even before I started oestrogen, as soon as I started identifying as transfem, I found a cornucopia of queer people, and for some reason, a lot more interest in me. I don't quite get it, cus it was ... a lot. For someone like me who was never really wanted by others (as far as i know at least), it seemed like I just needed to find my fellow queers. Suddenly I felt like I belonged, and I could feel that people wanted me there. It was weird.
